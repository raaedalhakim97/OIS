# THE OBSERVER WORLD · CHAPTER I · EPISODE 4 — "The Home of Five"
### Video script · ~2:00 · vertical 1080×1920 · (music by Magnus, synced by me)

**One idea.** Every note has a home in the scale. The viewer absorbs a whole scale + a
pulse through pure story — no lesson.

**Logline.** The song broke into five notes, lost across the worlds. The Keeper travels
through dawn, morning, golden day, dusk and winter, **talks to each note** (call-and-
response), and brings it home to its step in the scale. The last note (F, the root) is
frozen and afraid and won't be dragged — so he plays the others and makes a home worth
coming to, and it comes on its own. The scale runs whole; the world sings.

**Visual grammar (consistent across the series).** Gold light = home/whole · cold-blue =
lost · the **staircase of light = the scale** (five rising steps) · **♪ above a head =
singing now** · the Keeper's lantern = his voice.

**Music (Magnus):** piano = the Keeper · the notes answer · **high trembling violin = the
winter danger** · soft breathing heartbeat = the pulse · everything resolves warm at the
end (violin joins the piano). See the Magnus prompt + cue sheet.

Night, the home hill. Beside the Keeper: **five dark, empty steps** (the scale, waiting).

## NOTE 1 · the Restless — dawn · 0:16–0:34
Dawn (warm horizon). A restless note (**G**) paces a ridge. **The Keeper calls** (lantern
sings, ♪) — **the note answers** (♪) — and follows him. **G takes the 2nd step** and rings.
**Caption:** `the first was restless —` / `always somewhere else.`

## NOTE 2 · the Bright — morning · 0:34–0:52
Morning meadow. A bright note (**A**) spins alone. The Keeper **calls, it answers**; **A
takes its step.** Two steps glow.
**Caption:** `the second was bright,` / `and lonely inside it.`

## NOTE 3 · the Strong — golden day · 0:52–1:08
Golden hour. A strong note (**C**) guards a small dim light and won't leave it. The Keeper
**calls, it answers**, and brings *both*; **C takes its step.** Three steps glow.
**Caption:** `the third would not come` / `without the one it kept.`

## NOTE 4 · the Dreamer — dusk · 1:08–1:22
Dusk, first stars. A wistful note (**D**) gazes at the sky. The Keeper sits, **calls softly,
it answers**; **D takes its step.** Four steps glow — only the top is dark.
**Caption:** `the fourth was a dreamer —` / `it only needed company.`

## THE LOW POINT · the danger — winter · 1:22–1:45
Winter — snow, cold, wind. The last note (**F**, the root) is **frozen and afraid**, curled
small, almost out. The Keeper reaches — it **shrinks away**. He pulls; it dims more. He
stops. **[MUSIC: high trembling violin — the danger/defence tension; the pulse thins.]**
**Caption:** `the last one was afraid.` *(beat)* `and you cannot` / `carry a note home.`

## THE TURN & PAYOFF — back home, night→first light · 1:45–1:58
At the home hill, with the four seated, the Keeper **lets the home sing** — the four steps
call a warm phrase into the dark. Across the snow, the frozen note lifts… and **comes on
its own**, drawn by a home worth coming to. It climbs the **top step (F, the root)** — the
staircase is **whole, five steps of light.** The **scale runs up and down**; the world
sings; the sky warms toward dawn. **[MUSIC: piano melody returns; the violin comes home and
sings in harmony — reconciled; warm F-major finish.]**
**Caption:** `so he played the others —` / `and made a home worth coming to.` *(then)*
`and the last note` / `came on its own.`

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- **Motion:** calm — small breathe, gentle swing, clean walking. **No bounce.**
- **Backgrounds change per scene:** night · dawn · morning · golden day · dusk · winter.
- **Staircase = the scale** (5 rising steps; each lights + a note lands on it, low→high).
- **Keeper communicates** — a ♪ over the Keeper (call) then ♪ over the note (answer) at
  every meeting.
- **Captions centered, with a shadow** so they read on the bright winter/morning skies.
- **Sync:** Magnus delivers the MP3; I cut the animation to match it (the five note-landings
  + the winter violin + the payoff run are the sync anchors).
