# THE OBSERVER WORLD · CHAPTER I · EPISODE 3 — "The Home of Five"
### Reworked shooting script · ~2:00 · vertical 1080×1920

**The pitch.** A music-learning series that never *teaches* — it tells a story, and the
music learns you. This episode, the viewer absorbs a whole **scale** and a **pulse**
without being told a single rule: they just watch the Keeper bring five lost notes home,
one at a time, and hear the scale build under them.

**Logline.** The Silence scattered the song into five notes, each lost in a different
world. The Keeper walks through night, dawn, morning, and winter to find them and bring
each one home to its seat in the scale — but the last note, frozen and afraid, will not
be dragged. He learns you don't force a note home; you make a home worth coming to. He
plays the four, and the fifth comes on its own. The scale is whole. The world sings again.

**What the viewer learns (invisibly).**
- **The scale** — five notes (the home of five: **F · G · A · C · D**, the F pentatonic —
  the "no wrong notes" scale, perfect for beginners) seat themselves as **rising steps of
  light**, sounding their pitch as they land: the ear learns the scale, low→high.
- **The pulse / beat** — a soft heartbeat starts with the first note and **each new note
  adds a layer**, so a simple groove builds: the viewer feels meter without counting.
- **Note character** — each note has a personality and a home time, so they're memorable
  (the restless one, the bright one, the strong one, the dreamer, the frozen one).

**Craft spine.** Clear goal (bring five home so the world can sing) · rising difficulty
(each note harder) · a real low point (the fifth refuses) · a turn that *is* the theme
(don't drag it — call it) · earned payoff (the scale sings) · a button + question.

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- **MOTION FIX (important).** Drop the bouncy body-dip and the springy overshoot. Keep only:
  a **small breathe** (subtle), the **gentle swing** (lean rock with the moment), and clean
  **walking**. Calm and readable — never bouncing.
- **BACKGROUNDS CHANGE PER SCENE** (new `world.py` palettes): **night · dawn · morning ·
  golden day · dusk · winter** — each its own sky, light, and color, so every scene feels
  like a new place and time. (Snow/wind for winter.)
- **THE STAIRCASE OF LIGHT = the scale.** Five rising steps; each lights and sounds its
  pitch as a note seats — the scale is taught to the eye and ear at once. Ends with the
  run up/down.
- **THE PULSE builds.** One heartbeat → +quarter pulse → +off-beat → +low layer → full
  groove → thins at the low point → returns at the payoff. Meter, felt not counted.
- **Scenes are short and snappy** (~14–18s), quick and warm — keeps the scroller.
- **Note personalities** make the scale memorable (restless G, bright A, strong C, dreamer
  D, afraid F).
