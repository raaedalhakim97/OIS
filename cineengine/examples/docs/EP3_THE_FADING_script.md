# THE OBSERVER WORLD · CHAPTER I · EPISODE 3 — "The Fading"
### Shooting script · ~2:00 · vertical 1080×1920

**Logline.** At the edge of the Home Fields, where the Silence creeps in, a light is
going out. The Keeper races to reach it — and learns a soul doesn't fade from the
dark, it fades from being *alone*. He can't save it by pouring in his own light: a
note cannot complete itself. Only another note, answering, can pull it back. He wakes
a second soul; the two hold a perfect fifth; the fading light returns, and the
Silence loses a step of ground.

**Land / territory.** *the Fading Edge* — the frontier of the Home Fields where the
Silence begins. (New territory introduced this episode.)

**Note logic.** Fading soul = **D** (the Dreamer, 62), sinking. Rescue = **G** (55)
answering → a **perfect fifth** (G–D): strength, loyalty, the backbone bond that pulls
someone back from the edge.

**Theme / rule (spoken).** *A note cannot complete itself — not even the Keeper can.
Only another note, answering.*

**New lore this episode.** The **Silence** becomes an active, visible threat (an
encroaching void that unmakes light) → good vs. evil, real stakes. A **ticking-clock
challenge** (reach the light before it goes out) → action, kept in the calm style.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (standards applied)
- **Runtime** ~2:00; **captions centered**; **breathing** pace; **title card** intro.
- **Note-glyphs** above the speaker/singer (♪, and ♫ for the fifth), rising + fading.
- **The Silence** = a moving dark mass at the frame edge that advances during Beats 1–3
  and **retreats** on the rescue (Beat 6) — animated, legible, the visible antagonist.
- **The clock** = D's light literally shrinking + the dark advancing = felt urgency,
  no timers or UI, keeps the calm aesthetic.
- **Music is the plot.** Fading = a lone, flattening, quieting D. Rescue = G answering
  into a **perfect fifth** (strength/loyalty), grounded by a low root, then bloom.
- **Palette.** Home-field night (indigo/blue, grass, stars); the Silence a colder,
  starless black; fading light pale-cold → warm gold on rescue.
- **The Keeper never uses his own note** — he *connects* (wakes G, turns it to D). This
  honors the world-rule and keeps his true note a mystery.
- **World consequence + meta.** Every rescue = the Silence loses one step of ground; the
  Keeper learns he cannot be the answer, only find it — a step in his own arc.
