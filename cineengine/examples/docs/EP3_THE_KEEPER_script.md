# THE OBSERVER WORLD · CHAPTER I · EPISODE 3 — "The Keeper"
### The adventure begins · Shooting script · ~2:05 · vertical 1080×1920

This episode **introduces the main character** and **starts the journey**. From here
the series is his adventure across the Observer World. "The Fading" (previous draft)
becomes **Episode 4** — the first trial on the road; E3 ends by hooking into it.

---

## MAIN CHARACTER — the Keeper (the Lightkeeper)
- **Who.** The protagonist. A small cloaked silhouette who carries a glowing lantern
  (his orb). His **true name and note are hidden** — the series' central mystery (the
  finale reveals he is the tonic that everyone was tuning toward). Called simply **the
  Keeper**.
- **The lantern.** Holds the **last ember of the Great Chord** — a living memory of
  harmony. It stirs when real harmony is near; it is his compass.
- **Origin / wound.** He was **one note inside a perfect chord** — a home, a family of
  notes (his chord-kin). The **Silence** scattered them across the world. He was left
  holding the last light.
- **Mission (the adventure).** Walk the world; help fading and incomplete souls find the
  notes that complete them. **Every soul completed restores a piece of the Great Chord,
  pushes the Silence back one step, and draws him nearer his lost kin.**
- **Rule / limit.** He **cannot complete himself**, and he **cannot be someone else's
  answer** — he can only *observe* who a soul is, hear what's missing, and **find and
  connect the ones who answer each other.** (Keeps him humble; makes every story about
  connection, not rescue-by-hero.)
- **Powers.** Observation (hears a soul's note + what's missing); the lantern (wakes,
  steadies, and guides other lights, and echoes harmony); his signature **weighted
  swing** (he rocks with feeling; his light swings like a lantern).
- **Voice.** Quiet, wise, kind, unhurried — carrying grief and hope at once.
- **Arc.** Grieving & still & alone → the one who rebuilds the song by giving others
  theirs → the root of the Great Chord's return.

## PRODUCTION NOTES (standards applied)
- ~2:05; **captions centered**; **breathing** pace; **title card** intro; **note-glyphs**.
- **The lantern-chord** is a new signature image: the Great Chord living inside the glass,
  the emotional core of the main character. Reusable across the series.
- **The Silence** appears as the memory of the scattering (Beat 2) and looms at the hook
  (Beat 7) — the antagonist established.
- **The horizon / map reveal** (Beat 6) launches the adventure geography — set up the
  territories so future episodes are "stops" on the road.
- **Music is the plot.** Whole chord (home) → fracture into dissonance (the Silence) →
  lone note (grief) → distant call-and-response (the world still sings) → warm resolve
  (the mission) → hopeful ringing step (departure).
