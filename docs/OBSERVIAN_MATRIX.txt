1 · THE SEVEN SOUNDS
==========================================================================

Every word in the language is built from these. Do = middle C.

  SOUND   DEGREE  MIDI      HZ   MEANING CORE
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Do           0    60   261.6   Origin — self, beginning, existence
  Re           2    62   293.7   Motion — change, travel
  Mi           4    64   329.6   Reflection — the other, memory
  Fa           5    65   349.2   Question — doubt, the unknown
  Sol          7    67   392.0   Harmony — agreement, connection
  La           9    69   440.0   Emotion — feeling, grief, love
  Ti          11    71   493.9   Future — what is coming, tension

2 · GRAMMAR — THE FOUR AXES
==========================================================================

Meaning is not only WHICH notes. It is how they are placed.

  RHYTHM AND FEELING (style)
    STYLE          NOTE      GAP   LOUD   RHYTHM
    ------------------------------------------------------------------
    calm           0.6s     0.3s   0.11   even
    urgent        0.26s    0.09s   0.12   even
    fear           0.3s   uneven    0.1   broken — fear will not keep time
    confident     0.42s    0.16s   0.12   even
    whisper       0.55s    0.32s  0.055   even

  REGISTER — who is speaking
    -12   an Elder, or something vast and old
      0   an ordinary voice
    +12   excitement, a child, a small note

  COLOUR — hope or grief
    major   as written
    minor   Mi and La drop a semitone. Grief bends exactly the two sounds
            that mean 'the other' and 'feeling'.

  SILENCE
    A rest is a word. The Silence has no notes of its own, so it answers
    with absence.

5 · READING THE GRAMMAR
==========================================================================

  - "i was wrong" = Do Fa Sol — self, then doubt, then MOVING INTO
    harmony. An apology is a journey, and the language makes you walk
    it.

  - "no" = Do+Fa — self and doubt sounded TOGETHER, as a clash.
    Disagreement is dissonance, not a word.

  - "we are connected" = Do+Mi+Sol — self, other and harmony at once. A
    complete thought is a chord.

  - "the same" = Mi Mi — the other, twice. The Silence's favourite
    sentence: an echo is not an answer.

6 · PRODUCTION RULE
==========================================================================

  Captions carry the meaning. Observian carries the speech. Characters
  never get written dialogue — the lantern sings, the caption
  translates. Recurring phrases repeat until the audience knows them
  without being told.

  In the trailers this became call and response: Alan speaks, and the
  world answers in Observian. That is the premise demonstrated rather
  than described.
