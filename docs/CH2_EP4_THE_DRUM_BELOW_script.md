# CHAPTER II · EPISODE 4 — "The Drum Below"
**Land:** the Drum Below · **Runtime:** ~2:20 · **Spoken:** Observian only

## LOGLINE
The Hollow can now copy any pitch — so pitch is useless against it. But it cannot keep
**time**. The world's heartbeat begins to stumble: something is beating *between* the
beats. The trio descends to the Drum Below and finds the Hollow standing on the heart of
the world, striking it out of time. Their strikes only land **on the beat** — and the
between-stone, who lives between the steps, must learn that **the beat has no between**.

## THE HIDDEN LESSON
**The beat: downbeat vs off-beat, and counting 1-2-3-4.** On-beat strikes land; off-beat
strikes dissipate. The audience learns to feel "the one" by watching hits succeed or fail.
(Continuity: the world's heartbeat is the same **72 bpm** established in Ch I Ep6.)

## THE SEASON PATTERN (why this escalates)
Ep3 the Hollow learned **pitch** (it sang their fifth back perfectly).
Ep4 it learns **time** — the hook is that it never came to break the drum. It came to
*learn the tempo*. Every victory teaches it something. That is the shape of Chapter II.
