### Register — who is speaking

| Shift | Voice |
|---|---|
| `-12` | an Elder, or something vast and old |
| `0` | an ordinary voice |
| `+12` | excitement, a child, a small note |

### Colour — hope or grief

| Colour | Effect |
|---|---|
| `major` | as written |
| `minor` | **Mi and La drop a semitone.** Grief bends exactly the two sounds that mean *the other* and *feeling* |

### Silence

A rest is a word. The Silence has no notes of its own, so it answers with absence.

## 5 · Reading the grammar

Why the notes are not arbitrary:


- **"i was wrong" = `Do Fa Sol`** — self, then doubt, then *moving into* harmony. An apology is a journey, and the language makes you walk it.
- **"no" = `Do+Fa`** — self and doubt sounded *together*, as a clash. Disagreement is dissonance, not a word.
- **"we are connected" = `Do+Mi+Sol`** — self, other and harmony at once. A complete thought is a chord.
- **"the same" = `Mi Mi`** — the other, twice. The Silence's favourite sentence: an echo is not an answer.


## 6 · Production rule

Captions carry the meaning. Observian carries the speech. Characters never get written dialogue — the lantern sings, the caption translates. Recurring phrases repeat until the audience knows them without being told.


In the trailers this became call and response: Alan speaks, and the world answers in Observian. That is the premise demonstrated rather than described.
