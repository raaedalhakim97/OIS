# CHAPTER II · EPISODE 6 — "The Chord of Three"
**Land:** the Long Dark · **Runtime:** ~2:24 · **Spoken:** Observian only

## LOGLINE
The thing walking toward them arrives — not a Hollow. **The Sunderer.** He sings their
First Song back perfectly: in tune *and* in time, because he has learned both. One note
cannot stop him. Two notes cannot stop him — he learned the fifth in Ep3. So they sound
**three at once: a chord.** And it holds, because a chord is the one thing he cannot
copy — **he is alone, and a chord cannot be made alone.** So he does not break the
chord. He breaks the three: he takes the Elder, who has no note left to defend himself.

## THE HIDDEN LESSON
**The triad.** One note = a pitch. Two = an interval. **Three = a chord** — and a chord
is a thing that only exists between beings. The audience learns *why* harmony needs
more than one voice, and hears one → two → three stack up as the shield grows.

## THE VILLAIN'S WEAKNESS IS THEMATIC
He took his note back and kept it. So he has pitch, he has time, he has power — and
**nobody to sound with.** The being who refused to give is the one being who can never
make a chord. That is the whole series' philosophy weaponised.

## CONTINUITY
- **Ep3** taught the fifth and ended with him singing it back — here it fails, exactly as promised.
- **Ep4** taught the beat and ended with him walking away in time — here he arrives in time.
- **Ep5** revealed the Elder has nothing left — here that is why he is the one taken.
- Sets the finale: three became two, and the last thing the Sunderer cannot copy is **a rest**.
