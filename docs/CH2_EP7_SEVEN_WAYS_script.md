# CHAPTER II · EPISODE 7 — "Seven Ways to Stand Together"
**Land:** the Long Dark · **Runtime:** ~2:30 · **Spoken:** Observian only

## LOGLINE
Two are not enough to make a chord. So the Keeper stops trying to fight *with* the notes
and starts **calling them** — and discovers that the same seven notes can stand together
in **seven different ways**. Each chord is a different weapon with a different feeling,
and he finds them one at a time, in the middle of the fight. The last discovery is not a
chord at all: it is **two chords in a row** — the pull home — and that is the one thing
the Sunderer cannot follow, because he has no home to return to.

## THE HIDDEN LESSON
**Triads on every degree of the scale**, and then **the cadence.**
Seven notes → seven chords → and a progression that *resolves*.

| # | Chord | Notes | Feeling / effect in the fight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Do Mi Sol** (C major) | 1-3-5 | home, bright — **he copies it instantly. it fails.** |
| 2 | **Re Fa La** (D minor) | 2-4-6 | strange, sad — he hesitates. it *works.* |
| 3 | **Fa La Do** (F major) | 4-6-1 | shelter — wide and warm, it pushes him back |
| 4 | **La Do Mi** (A minor) | 6-1-3 | grief — **the Elder's colour.** the hardest to sound |
| 5 | **Ti Re Fa** (B diminished) | 7-2-4 | the unstable one — it sounds like *him*, and it hurts both sides |
| 6 | **Sol Ti Re** (G major) | 5-7-2 | it does not push. **it pulls.** |
| 7 | **Sol Ti Re → Do Mi Sol** | V → I | **THE CADENCE.** the pull home. he cannot follow. |

## THE MECHANIC
The Keeper **conducts**. He calls note-names and the notes on the staff light and sound
together; beams run from each lit note down into a single converging strike. Wrong
combinations fizzle. Right ones land — each with its own colour:
**major = gold · minor = violet · diminished = sick green · dominant = white-hot.**

## BEATS

**ACT I — two is not a chord *(0:00–0:30)***
> *there were two of them now.* · **K:** together. → Do + Mi only — a bare third, and it **fails**.
> *two notes are not a chord.* · *they are only a question.*
> Then the Keeper looks up at the staff. **K:** listen. · *so he called the scale itself.*

**ACT II — the discoveries *(0:31–1:38)***
> **Do Mi Sol** — gold, huge — and he sings it back before it lands. *"he already knew that one."*
> **Re Fa La** — violet. He **hesitates.** *"a chord he had never heard."* It lands.
> **Fa La Do** — wide gold. *"shelter."* It pushes him back a full step.
> **La Do Mi** — deep violet. *"this one was the Elder's colour."* The hardest to sound; it costs them.
> **Ti Re Fa** — sick green, unstable. *"a chord that stands on nothing."* It hurts him — and them.
> *"seven notes.* · *and seven ways to stand together."*

**ACT III — the cadence *(1:39–2:10)***
> **Sol Ti Re** — white-hot. *"this one did not push."* · *"it pulled."*
> → **Do Mi Sol.** The two chords in a row. The world **leans home.**
> *"every song wants to come home."* · *"he had nowhere to come home to."*
> He is dragged back and breaks away into the dark.

**ACT IV — the cost *(2:11–2:30)***
> *they had found seven ways to stand together.* · *and he had taken the one that mattered.*
> The Elder's place on the ground is empty. **K:** what now?
> *there was one chord left that they had never tried.* · *it had no notes in it at all.*
> → the finale: **the rest.**
