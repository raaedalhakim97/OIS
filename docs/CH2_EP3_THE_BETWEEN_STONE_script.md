# CHAPTER II · EPISODE 3 — "The Between-Stone"
**Land:** the Fading Edge · **Runtime:** ~2:20 · **Spoken:** Observian only (captions translate)

---

## LOGLINE
La lies trapped a half step below itself, in the crack *between* the steps — a place no
keeper can stand, because keepers walk only on the seven stones. The only one who can
go in is the outsider the Keeper turned away in Chapter I: **the sharp**. He must ask it
for help, hold the dark back with a perfect fifth, and let the between-stone bring La home.

## THE HIDDEN LESSON
**Enharmonics + the half step as a place.** A♭ and G♯ are *the same stone with two names*,
depending on which side you approach from. The note that seems like an outsider is native
to the very gap that traps a scale note. (Secondary: the perfect fifth as the strongest,
most stable interval — the "thread" from Ep10 — and consonance repelling the Hollow.)

## WHY THIS EPISODE MATTERS TO THE SERIES
It pays off the character planted in Ch I Ep9, who bowed and slipped away *"waiting for
another song."* This is that song. It also turns the duo into a **trio** — Keeper, Elder,
Sharp — the fellowship that will carry the rest of Chapter II.
